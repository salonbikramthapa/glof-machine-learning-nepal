# Glacial Lake Observatory annual lake inventories 2017–2024

Citation: Rawlins, L., Watson, C. S., Bhambri, R., Khadka, N., & Chand, M. B. (2026). Datasets supporting - Glacial Lake Observatory (GLO): A dataset of glacial lakes in Nepal and transboundary catchments (2017–2024) (v2) [Data set]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19370146

Source: https://zenodo.org/records/19370146
Version: v2, published 1 April 2026; data filenames v1.02.
Accessed: 28 September 2026.

The study's separate recent-change analysis uses `S2_20172024_NTB_GLOID_v1.02.gpkg`. Raw GeoPackages are not included in this repository. The provider documentation is retained in `PROVIDER_NOTES.md`.

`AREA_YEAR` identifies the observation year and `AREA` is in km². Polygon coordinates use ESRI:102025. GLO_ID and ICIMOD GL_ID belong to different identifier systems. The analysis compares provider GLO_ID values across years; it does not match those lakes to the historical ICIMOD classifier cohort. Missing years are not treated as zero lake area.
